- clouds in the sky
- fog, mist, sea fog, haze, or steam
- screen blur or haze caused by water droplets, condensation, rain, or a dirty/wet lens
- generally low-contrast, washed-out, or foggy frames; glare; or overexposure
- headlight or taillight beams or their reflections on wet roads (these are light, not smoke)
